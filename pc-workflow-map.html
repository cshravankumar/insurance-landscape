Why the support and compliance work exists

requirements and source references

The references below describe underwriting and claims records, reasons for decisions, and delegated-authority requirements. Examiners may review underwriting notes, and claim files must support the determination. The applicable requirements depend on the jurisdiction and agreement.

The law defines the "underwriting file"NAIC Ch.20: the file must include every input used to select, classify, rate, or terminate
Examiners read the underwriter's notesStandard 4 reviews "underwriter's file or notes on a system log"
Record reasons for declinationsrecord a valid reason for every declination. Monitor the declinations of agents also
Adverse decisions need stated reasonsNAIC #670: "specific and accurate reasons". FCRA: adverse-action notices on credit-driven pricing
Document the reasons for claim decisionsStandard 5: documentation "sufficient to support or justify the ultimate claim determination"
Record claim datesloss · reported · sufficient information · accepted or rejected, per claim
Identify the policy provisions behind a denialspecific policy provision and appeal instructions, mandated content
Lloyd’s delegated-authority categoriesFull → Pre-set Rates → No Discretion → Prior Submit. Prior Submit means referral of every risk
Delegated authority rarely covers claims declinationa coverholder "will very rarely have authority to decline a claim". Refer denials to the authorized decision-maker
MGA oversight is statutoryNAIC #225: semi-annual on-site review of underwriting and claims operations
Fronting carriers review underwriting proceduresdiligence items: how risks are "selected, priced, referred, and declined"
Bordereaux report premiums, risks, and claimspremium, risk and claims bordereaux, GPI tracking and aggregate reports by zone
If you use AI: keep the information and reasons that you need to review a decision. Check the relevant recordkeeping requirements before you change a workflow. Allianz’s announced work with Anthropic includes AI decision logging. See the company examples below.
