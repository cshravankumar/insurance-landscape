MGAs, MGUs & program administrators

1,100+ entities · $114B premium · +16% YoY

MGAs underwrite under authority delegated by carriers or fronting companies. Conning counts 850+ from statutory filings plus ~250 below the reporting threshold; globally there are 2,500+ MGA enterprises. 60% of US MGA premium comes from shops under $500M. Independent MGAs (46.6% of premium) write more premium than carrier-owned MGAs. The largest MGA groups by global revenue are owned by brokers — Brown & Brown ($1B+ MGA revenue, world #1), TIH/CRC, Amwins, Ryan Specialty, and Gallagher are the top five. Further AI sells software to companies in this segment.
