Reading this honestly
This is each company's latest known round, not its full funding history — a company that raised in 2020 and again in 2024 shows up once, in 2024. 36 of 332 companies (11%) have no parseable date in their funding text and are excluded here entirely (they're still in the totals on the Capital Flow page). Treat this as a directional read of when capital arrived, not an audited fundraising calendar.

Source: insurtech-dataset.json (332 companies), same repo as Workflow Evidence Map and Investor Graph. Dates parsed from each company's free-text funding description (month+year where stated, YC batch codes as an approximate quarter, bare year as a fallback) — a best-effort extraction, not a structured rounds database. Investor "deal count" is not dollar-attributed: when a round has multiple investors, the round's dollar amount can't be split among them from this data, so the heatmap counts participations, not capital deployed per investor.
