Insurance Landscape · US P&C

Who's out there, how the work flows, and where the money went

Five linked maps built from public sources, each with its sourcing one click away. Start with the market map if you want the players; the workflow map if you want the process; the capital pages if you want the money.

Market MapEvery participant type in the $1.05T US P&C market — carriers, reinsurers, brokers, wholesalers, MGAs, fronts, claims, and the periphery — named, sized, and audited against canonical censuses.reference · ~500 companies Workflow Evidence MapSix roles, 158 tasks with cited evidence, and an automation grade per stage: what a broker, underwriter, MGA, and adjuster actually do all day, and which parts AI has reached.evidence · 6 roles × 158 tasks Investor Graph332 insurance startups and their investors as a browsable directory — click an investor to light up its portfolio, click a company for who backs it.directory · 332 startups Capital FlowDisclosed funding laid along the value chain — broker → underwriter → policy in force → claims — plus the investors who concentrated in one tier versus spread across all of them.analysis · $13B+ disclosed Capital TimelineThe same data with a time axis: when the money arrived, into which tier, from which investors — cross-filterable, drillable to the company.analysis · 2016–2026
How the five fit together. The market map is the roster. The workflow map is the spine — its participants and stages are the categories every other page uses. The investor graph is the startup layer sitting on that spine. The two capital pages put dollars on it.
Sources, method, and the data behind every page are in the repository: insurtech-dataset.json (one record per company, with a source URL and verification date on each funding figure), census/ (the exhibitor and directory lists the roster was audited against), and analysis/ (the parser, the capital analysis, and a weekly freshness probe). Figures are best-effort readings of public announcements — floors, not audited totals. Compiled 2026-09.
