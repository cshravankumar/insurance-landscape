Insurance Landscape · US P&C

The participants, the work, and the capital of US P&C insurance

This site has five linked maps from public sources. Each map gives its sources. Start with the Market Map to see the participants. Use the Workflow Evidence Map to see the work. Use the capital pages to see the capital.

Market MapThis map names each type of participant in the $1.05T US P&C market. The types include carriers, reinsurers, brokers, wholesalers, MGAs, fronting carriers, claims companies and service companies. It gives their sizes and compares the lists with industry directories.reference · 378 company entries Workflow Evidence MapThis map shows six roles and 158 tasks with cited evidence. It shows the daily work of a broker, an underwriter, an MGA and an adjuster. Each stage has an automation grade.evidence · 6 roles, 158 tasks Investor GraphThis directory lists 831 insurtech companies and their investors. Click an investor to show its companies. Click a company to show its investors.directory · 831 companies Capital FlowThis page puts the disclosed capital on the value chain, from broker to underwriter, to policy in force, to claims. It also shows the investors with a concentration in one tier.analysis · $20.8B disclosed Capital TimelineThis page shows the same data by year. For each year it shows the rounds, their tier and their investors. You can filter the chart and open the list of companies.analysis · 2010 to 2026
How the five maps go together. The Market Map is the list of participants. The Workflow Evidence Map gives the participants and the stages. All the other pages use them as categories. The Investor Graph puts the insurtech companies in those categories. The two capital pages add the capital.
The sources, the method and the data of each page are in the repository. The file insurtech-dataset.json has one record for each company, with a source URL and a verification date for each capital amount. The folder census/ has the exhibitor lists and the directories. The review compared the list of companies with them. The folder analysis/ has the parser, the capital analysis and a weekly check of the age of the data. The amounts are estimates from public announcements. They are minimum amounts, not audited totals. Compiled 2026-11.
